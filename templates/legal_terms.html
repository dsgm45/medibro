1. What MediBro Is

MediBro is a patient engagement and care-coordination platform with EHR-lite record-keeping. It is not a full Electronic Medical Record (EMR) or Electronic Health Record (EHR) system, and it is not a diagnostic tool. It does not replace clinical judgment, laboratory testing, imaging, or in-person examination.

2. No Doctor-Patient Relationship by Default

Creating an account, browsing doctor profiles, or sending a message through MediBro does not, by itself, establish a doctor-patient relationship. A doctor-patient relationship is established only once a specific appointment has been booked and accepted between a patient and a doctor on the platform.

3. AI-Assisted Features

MediBro's Symptom Checker and AI Health Chat use an AI model (currently Google's Gemini) to provide general health guidance. This guidance is generated automatically, is not reviewed by a medical professional before being shown to you, and is not a diagnosis. Certain messages containing emergency-related language are intercepted by a separate, deterministic rule-based system before ever reaching the AI - this is described further in the Medical Disclaimer.

4. SOS and Emergency Contacts

The SOS feature lets you log an emergency alert and displays your saved emergency contacts' phone numbers for you to call. MediBro does not itself contact emergency services, does not monitor SOS alerts in real time, and does not guarantee that any emergency contact will see or respond to an alert. In a genuine emergency, always contact local emergency services directly.
