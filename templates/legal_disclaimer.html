Medical Disclaimer

Last updated: {{ current_year }}

⚠ If you are experiencing a medical emergency, do not rely on this platform. Call your local emergency number immediately.

AI Output Is Not a Diagnosis

Guidance generated by the Symptom Checker or AI Health Chat is general in nature. It does not name specific medical conditions, does not recommend specific medications or dosages, and is not reviewed by a doctor before being shown to you. It should never be treated as a diagnosis or as a substitute for professional medical advice.

Emergency Detection Is a Supplement, Not a Guarantee

MediBro uses a deterministic, rule-based check to recognize certain emergency-related language (for example, chest pain, difficulty breathing, or language associated with a mental health crisis) before any message reaches the AI. This is a safety layer, not a guarantee - it may not catch every possible way of describing an emergency, and it is not a substitute for seeking real medical help when something feels seriously wrong. When in doubt, seek help directly rather than waiting on a response from this platform.

SOS and Emergency Contacts Have Real Limitations

The SOS feature logs an alert and displays your saved emergency contacts' numbers for you to call yourself. It does not notify anyone automatically, does not contact emergency services on your behalf, and does not guarantee anyone will see or respond to it. Treat it as a convenience for reaching people you trust, not as a monitored emergency response system.

Vitals and Medicine Tracking Are Not Medical Devices

The vitals you log (blood pressure, heart rate, SpO2, temperature) are self-reported figures you enter manually, not readings from a connected or certified medical monitoring device. Medicine reminders are a convenience feature and do not replace instructions from your doctor or pharmacist. Do not use trends shown in this app as the sole basis for a medical decision.

Always Consult a Real Doctor

For diagnosis, treatment, medication changes, or anything involving your ongoing care, consult a licensed doctor - either through MediBro's appointment booking or in person. Nothing on this platform is a substitute for that relationship.
