How MediBro Keeps AI Safe

Anything that sounds like a medical emergency is caught by fixed, rule-based logic before AI is ever involved - the AI never makes that call. If the AI is ever unavailable, MediBro falls back to safe, rule-based guidance instead of guessing. Nothing here replaces your doctor - it's designed to help you get to one, safely.
